# Electromagnetic Storm — whole-map terrain

Status: **planned 2026-11-06; R1–R4 ruled by the user the same day** (section 5). D5 onward are defaults built
unless told otherwise.

---

## 1. The rule

User's text (2026-11-06):

> These storms are often caused by sunspots, but also occur in certain nebulae. They produce random electromagnetic
> (EM) effects that vary from unit to unit. If an EM storm is active, it will affect every unit in the scenario unless
> otherwise noted. At the start of each turn, before Initial Orders, each unit suffers the following effects. Note that
> these effects are temporary and end at the conclusion of the current turn.
> - Ships suffer a penalty of 1d6 from their sensor ratings. Fighters and shuttles lose 1d3 from their offensive
>   bonuses, but never more than one-half (drop fractions) of their offensive rating.
> - All units suffer a penalty of 2d6 from their initiative ratings.
> - Ships might temporarily lose power. Roll 1d6, and if a 4 or less is rolled (5 or less for enormous units, 3 or
>   less for medium ships), reduce the ship's power by the number of points shown on the die.
> - Ships suffer "attacks" by the EM storm against their exposed systems. Medium ships are attacked once, heavy combat
>   vessels 1d2 times, capital ships 1d3 times, and enormous units 1d6 times. Roll each attack against a random side
>   (but do not count a destroyed side as a valid target). The attack always hits, so roll for location against the
>   affected side. If structure is hit, there is no effect. However, any other system will be involuntarily
>   deactivated exactly as if it had been hit by an electro-burst beam. Multiple hits on the same location in the same
>   turn are not cumulative. (Ships with EM shields may elect to accept this effect using a facing shield, but this
>   must be declared before the roll for location is made. If such a ship is hit by more than one EM storm "attack" in
>   the same turn, it cannot use the same shield against more than one attack.)
> - Advanced races (factionAge 3 or more) subtract 1 from all effect rolls. They do not temporarily lose power and are
>   immune to the "attacks" generated by the storm.

User's additions: the EM shield choice reuses the Meteor Defence way of declaring (a button on the system), for EM
shields only. May be combined with a Nebula: the sensor penalty is then skipped (there is no EW anyway), and the
fighter OB penalty comes off the nebula-halved figure. Mines and terrain are immune. A rare option - gate everything.

## 2. Shape

- **Rules key `emStorm: 1` -> `EMStormRule`** (NebulaRule's shape). No unit, no hexes.
- **Server gate `TacGamedata::$emStormPresent`**, ASSIGNED both ways in `TacGamedata::__construct`. Every reader tests
  it first; an ordinary game pays one false static read per site.
- **Client gate `gamedata.rules.emStorm`** via `window.emStorm.isActive()` (client/emStorm.js, game.php only - lobby
  code guards with `window.emStorm &&`).
- **The Energy Draining Field is the template** (handlers/EdfExposure.php): a sweep that rolls everything for the
  coming turn and writes each result as a `oneturn` critical carrying its amount in `param`. `hasCritical` /
  `sumCriticalParam` report a oneturn crit only on `crit->turn + 1`, so a crit stamped turn N is in force for exactly
  turn N+1 and lapses by itself. The RESULT is the record: replay and reloads read the crits, no roll is re-made.
- **New crit classes, not the EDF's**: EdfExposure::accumulate reads last turn's crit of the SAME class as its running
  total, so a storm crit of an EDF class would corrupt the EDF's escalation.

### When it rolls (R1)

| Sweep | Called from | Stamp | In force | Who |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| End of turn | top of `Criticals::setCriticals` (beside the EDF) | turn N | turn N+1 | every unit on the map |
| Deployment | `DeploymentGamePhase::advance` | turn T-1 | turn T | every unit deployed THIS turn (turn 1: everyone) |

The end-of-turn sweep runs before Pass 1, so an attack's forced critical roll (R2) is rolled by Pass 1 itself. The
Deployment sweep rolls those itself (`testCritical`). Idempotent per unit per stamp turn via the initiative crit
(every stormed unit gets one: 2d6, or 2d6-1, is never 0).

## 3. Effect map

| # | Effect | Rolled | Stored as | Read by (server / client) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sensors -1d6 (ships; not in a nebula) | d6 (-1 adv.) | `EMStormSensors` on the Scanner | `Scanner::getOutput` + stripForJson's `edfDrain` / none - `systems.getOutput` already subtracts `edfDrain` |
| 2 | Fighter/shuttle OB -1d3, max floor(OB/2) | d3 (-1 adv.), capped at roll time on the (nebula-halved) OB | `EMStormOffensive` on the sample fighter | `Weapon::calculateHitBase` / `weaponManager.computeOEW` (MIRROR PAIR) + OB tooltips |
| 3 | Initiative -2d6 (all units) | 2d6 (-1 adv.) | `EMStormInitiative` on CnC / sample fighter (x5 FV) | `BaseShip::getCommonIniModifiers` / none |
| 4 | Power loss (ships, not adv.) | d6 <= 3/4/5 (medium/heavy+capital/enormous) | `EMStormPower` on the Reactor | `Reactor::getOutput` + stripForJson's `edfDrain` / none - `power.js` already subtracts `edfDrain` |
| 5 | Attacks (ships, not adv.) | count by size; side; hit chart | `ForcedOfflineOneTurn` (stamp turn) or a forced crit roll at +4 | the existing Burst Beam paths |
| 6 | EM shield takes an attack | declared | IndividualNote `stormDefence` on the shield | `EMStorm` sweep / `emStorm.js` + Storm Defence button |
| 7 | Combat log | - | technical fire order, damageclass `EMStorm` | `Firing::isHyperspaceLogOrder` skips it / `doShortLogText` |

`edfDrain` is the field the server already publishes for the EDF drain on Reactor and Scanner, and the client already
subtracts it. It now carries the sum of every one-turn drain the server applies, so sensors and power need no client
code.

### The attacks (R2, D12, D16)
1. Count: medium (LCV, MCV, OSAT - shipSizeClass <= 1) 1, heavy 1d2, capital 1d3, `Enormous` 1d6.
2. Side: a random outer section (distinct `loc` from `getLocations()`) whose Structure is alive; none -> no attack.
3. A declared, unused, live EM shield whose arc covers the side's centre bearing takes it: forced offline for the turn.
   Several -> one at random (Burst Beam's choice).
4. Otherwise the side's hit chart, as a Standard hit from the side's centre bearing (`getHitSystem`, synthetic source):
   - Structure -> no effect;
   - a system already struck this storm -> no effect ("not cumulative");
   - Burst Beam's table: `powerReq > 0` or `canOffLine` -> ForcedOfflineOneTurn (EM hardening may resist); otherwise a
     forced critical roll at +4 (EM hardening -2). Advanced Armor -> no effect.

## 5. Decisions

**User rulings (2026-11-06):**
- **R1 Timing.** End of the previous turn (the Critical step) and, for turn 1 and late deployers, the end of the
  Deployment phase - so the effects are showing when Initial Orders opens. Storm Defence may be declared in Deployment
  as well, so the turn-1 storm can be shielded.
- **R2 Burst Beam exactly.** Switchable systems go offline for the turn; any other non-Structure system rolls a
  critical at +4 (a crit that results is permanent, like any other). Structure: no effect.
- **R3 Storm Defence is a STANDING ORDER** (amended after play-test game 4547; first built as "one storm per
  declaration"). Declared or withdrawn in Deployment or Initial Orders, it stands for every storm until withdrawn.
  Stored as changes (a `stormDefence` note, value 1 / 0, stamped turn + phase); the latest at or before a point decides.
- **R5 (game 4547) The log says what a forced critical roll produced** - " - critical: <description>" or " - no
  critical" - for the end-of-turn storm as well as the Deployment one.
- **R4 Static storm tint** on the map, hiding with the background like the nebula.

**Defaults (built unless told otherwise):**
- **D5** Advanced = the UNIT's factionAge >= 3: each effect roll -1 (sensors d6-1, OB d3-1, initiative 2d6-1, never
  below 0); no power loss; no attacks.
- **D6** Size: LCV/MCV/OSAT medium (1 attack, power on <= 3), HCV 1d2 / <= 4, capital 1d3 / <= 4, `Enormous` 1d6 / <= 5.
- **D7** Initiative 2d6 tabletop = x5 in FV's d100 initiative.
- **D8** OB cap = floor(OB / 2), OB taken after the nebula's halving (user) - worked out when rolled and stored capped.
- **D9** In a nebula the sensor roll is not made at all (user).
- **D10** Immune / skipped: mines, terrain, destroyed units, units not on the map (in hyperspace, docked, not yet
  deployed). Flights take OB and initiative only.
- **D11** EM hardening (Ipsha) gets its tabletop saves, as from any EM weapon (WeaponEM helpers): shutdown resisted on a
  d6 of 1, power loss -1, crit roll -2.
- **D12** A system struck twice in one storm suffers once; a shield absorbs one attack.
- **D13** Everyone sees the results (criticals, the combat log). A Storm Defence declaration is shown to its own side only.
- **D14** A power loss that the ship cannot shed by switching systems off blacks the reactor out - the EDF's
  `applyPowerBlackout`, generalised to read both drains. Otherwise the player powers down in Initial Orders as usual.
- **D15** One combat-log line per unit per storm, on its own Ramming Attack, headed "EM STORM:".
- **D16** The side's centre bearing = the width-weighted circular mean of its `getLocations()` arcs (MCV fore 0, aft 180).
- **D17** Sensors come off the first Scanner (the EDF's choice), power off the first Reactor (a starbase's per-section
  reactors: the first only).
- **D18** Meteor Defence is NOT renamed: Storm Defence is a sibling declaration on the shield, stored as a note (a
  shield is not a weapon, so the meteor-defence fire order does not fit it).

## 7. Traps found while planning

- `addCritical()` stamps `$gamedata->turn`; the Deployment sweep must build its crits with the stamp turn (T-1) itself.
- `Reactor::addCritical('ForcedOfflineOneTurn')` cascades to every powered system - only the blackout may reach it.
- A Deployment-advance log order is stamped with the CURRENT turn, so the Fire Phase gathers would fire it unless
  `Firing::isHyperspaceLogOrder` lists its damageclass (the Lucky Captain's precedent). Persist it with phase 2
  (`submitFireorders` keeps only ballistics in phase 1).
- `DeploymentGamePhase::advance` runs on the gamedata loaded BEFORE the last submitter's notes were saved - reload
  (inside the gate) or that player's Storm Defence is lost.
- The Deployment and Initial Orders declarations of turn 1 share a turn number: tell them apart by the note's PHASE.
- `isInArc` with a bearing of 360 is false (arch_server_bearing_360) - bearings are normalised to 0..359.
- A one-turn crit must never carry an `$outputMod` (effectCriticals sums outputMod with no turn filter).
